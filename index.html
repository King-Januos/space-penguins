ABOUT US

Our Mission & Why We Created It

Space Penguins was built to transform complex space engineering and resource management into an exciting, hands-on learning adventure. We created it to spark curiosity in STEM (Science, Technology, Engineering, and Math) by putting players right in the captain's chair of a lunar outpost.

How It Helps Young Explorers

The game teaches critical thinking, quick decision-making, and resource balancing by forcing commanders to weigh daily trade-offs between energy, oxygen, water, and radiation shielding.

Real-World Problem Solving

Just like real space missions, every choice matters. Players learn how fragile life support systems are, how to adapt during unexpected crises (like meteorite impacts), and how teamwork and engineering logic keep a crew alive.

How to Play

Run your lunar outpost by balancing life support, radiation shielding, power, and food production against engineering trade-offs.

• 15-Minute Timer: You have 15 minutes total to complete the 10-day mission.
• Daily Challenges: Press the base button when ready to face system trade-offs.
• Winning & Losing: Keep all resources above 0% and survive all 10 days to win.
